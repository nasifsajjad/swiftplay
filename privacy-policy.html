SwiftPlay – Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025  ·  Version 1.0

✓ No data collected  ·  No servers  ·  No tracking

Overview

SwiftPlay is a browser extension that automatically sets video playback speed and provides an on-screen speed control overlay. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data whatsoever.

What Data We Access

SwiftPlay reads and writes a single setting — your preferred playback speed — to chrome.storage.sync. This is a private Chrome API that stores your setting locally and optionally syncs it across your own Chrome profile. No third party (including the SwiftPlay developer) can access this data.

The extension does not read, record, or transmit:

· URLs or domains you visit
· Video titles, content, or watch history
· Browsing history or session data
· Any personally identifiable information
· Any analytics, telemetry, or usage statistics

Permissions Explained

storage — Used exclusively to save your chosen playback speed (a single number) via chrome.storage.sync.

scripting — Used to apply your speed preference to videos when you adjust it from the popup.

host_permissions (<all_urls>) — Required so the content script can inject the speed overlay on any website that contains a video element (e.g. YouTube, Netflix, Vimeo).

Third-Party Services

SwiftPlay does not communicate with any external server, API, or third-party service. There are no ads, no analytics, and no remote data collection of any kind.

Children's Privacy

SwiftPlay does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
